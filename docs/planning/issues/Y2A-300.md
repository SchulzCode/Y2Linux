## Goal / why this epic is needed

Represent the blueprint's native-audio gate before substantial Y2PlayerNative
audio development. Android bindings and HAL observations are reference evidence,
not a working Linux ALSA/ASoC path.

## Dependencies / evidence

Core clocks/pinctrl/I2C/IRQ/DMA and sufficient measured memory under the core
platform epic; supply ownership under #23/power epic; useful diagnostic capture.
Use `docs/knowledge/audio-path.md` and its later second-I2S/DL1 stock references.
Historical bus1 CS43131 at 0x30 and AW87559 at 0x58 identify research targets.
Verify board/revision and exact driver applicability before choosing bindings.

## High-level coverage

MT6582 AFE/ASoC CPU DAI, correct I2S instance, DMA/FIFO/IRQ, PLL/MCLK/BCLK/LRCK
and formats; codec I2C/reset/supplies; CS43131 upstream-driver applicability;
machine/DAI route; separate amplifier/speaker path, headphone detection/ACCDET,
mute/volume and pop-free sequencing. Capture firmware/calibration requirements
if evidence identifies any; do not presume a codec binding supplies SoC support.

## Exit evidence / unlocks

A real ALSA PCM device plays known samples to the actual intended output. Record
negotiated format/rate, channel correctness, safe level/routing, underruns,
repeated start/stop/idle-resume and error behavior. Supported rates and bit depths
are measured end-to-end, not inferred from a DAC specification. A tiny CLI/FFmpeg
proof precedes player architecture. Gate D unlocks serious native audio work;
speaker/high-resolution/Bluetooth may be separately declared follow-ups.

## Out-of-scope / stop

No player/decoder/DSP feature backlog or Android source port. Do not route audio
through PipeWire merely for wired playback. Stop at uncertain analog wiring,
unsafe gain/mute, clock/DMA mismatch or unverified supply/reset; no guessed live
ioctls or playback experiments. FM and Bluetooth transport are separate gates.

## Execution and validation boundary

High-level planning epic only, not an implementation task or hardware permission.
Expand only the next evidence-backed change when this phase is activated; preserve
existing stable IDs and link existing issues instead of duplicating them. No
speculative driver task list, build, flash or experiment is authorized by this epic.

Before closing the phase or proceeding through its exit gate, repeat the
[standing roadmap/gap audit](https://github.com/SchulzCode/Y2Linux/blob/main/docs/planning/roadmap-gap-audit.md#standing-milestone-boundary-rule)
using real hardware results. Separate owner reports, retained artifacts, source
support and actual device validation. Unknown dependencies stop the relevant
path; do not guess hardware or silently broaden scope. Milestone qualification
uses the appropriate full verification boundary; small later fixes keep targeted
validation and D08/package checks without routine provenance re-audits.
