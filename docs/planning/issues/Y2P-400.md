## Goal / why this epic is needed

Track battery-appliance viability beyond #23's narrow PMIC prerequisite study.
A stopped watchdog and inherited display do not establish safe long-lived power
management, charging, thermal control or suspend/resume.

## Dependencies / evidence

`docs/knowledge/power.md`, #23 and the active peripheral owners. Resolve duplicate
Android battery readings without converting service fields into physical truth.
Coordinate regulators/clocks with the core and audio epics before gating them.
Basic power/thermal reporting is needed in M2; it need not wait for every M3 task.

## High-level coverage

PMIC/PWRAP/regmap/regulator, gauge/charger identity, ADC/calibration and units;
battery/USB/charger reporting and safe limits; thermal sensors/cooling/trips;
power-key, orderly shutdown and reset; cpufreq/voltage/OPP, cpuidle, runtime PM,
suspend/resume, wake sources and rail retention. Cover screen-off wired audio,
SD/eMMC suspend safety, backlight, and later radio power-save integration.

## Exit evidence / unlocks

Trustworthy telemetry and bounded safety behavior first; then measured power
budgets in screen-on/off idle, wired playback, charging and suspend. Record wake
reliability, resume continuity, thermal behavior and playback/storage integrity.
Radio measurements wait for those controllers to work. Gate E establishes
battery practicality; thresholds must be chosen from real board evidence before
qualification, not invented in this epic.

## Out-of-scope / stop

No charger tuning, battery cycling, guessed OPP voltages or blanket clock/rail
gating. Unknown supply ownership, sensor calibration or lost wake/recovery stops
the affected experiment. This epic is not an instruction to implement all power
features at once or a claim they block early host-side application experiments.

## Execution and validation boundary

High-level planning epic only, not an implementation task or hardware permission.
Expand only the next evidence-backed change when this phase is activated; preserve
existing stable IDs and link existing issues instead of duplicating them. No
speculative driver task list, build, flash or experiment is authorized by this epic.

Before closing the phase or proceeding through its exit gate, repeat the
[standing roadmap/gap audit](https://github.com/SchulzCode/Y2Linux/blob/main/docs/planning/roadmap-gap-audit.md#standing-milestone-boundary-rule)
using real hardware results. Separate owner reports, retained artifacts, source
support and actual device validation. Unknown dependencies stop the relevant
path; do not guess hardware or silently broaden scope. Milestone qualification
uses the appropriate full verification boundary; small later fixes keep targeted
validation and D08/package checks without routine provenance re-audits.
