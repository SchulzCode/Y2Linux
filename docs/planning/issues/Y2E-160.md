## Type and goal

**Research.** Establish the minimum PMIC access and supply-ownership contract
needed to research input, display/backlight and removable SD safely.

## Why it comes next

The stock inventory names `pmic_mt6323`, but battery service readings conflict.
Rails and their control path are shared prerequisites; guessing them in each
peripheral driver would multiply risk. Audio will later reuse this evidence.

## Dependencies

Execution follows Y2E-155's decision; expanded RAM is not a technical requirement.
Use `power.md`, `y2-hardware.md`, retained platform metadata and the stock/FM
kernel. Depend on the known M1 diagnostic baseline, not a new hardware test.

## Exact scope

- Identify the bound PMIC/access transport and stock registration path, then
  compare that specific path with pinned upstream v6.18 bindings/drivers.
- Trace only named supply consumers for wheel/buttons, panel/backlight and SD;
  record confirmed rails/enable owners versus unknown board routes. Locate
  potential GPIO/pinctrl/clock prerequisites without implementing controllers.
- Explain duplicate battery fields from retained service evidence/code where
  available; assess only standard, side-effect-reviewed power_supply metadata
  for units/source validity. Do not label voltage/state trustworthy by name alone.
- Produce one dependency table and select the smallest future read-only identity
  or telemetry implementation, or name the specific missing transport/rail proof.

## Explicit out-of-scope

Charging limits or regulator writes, complete regulator/charger/gauge port,
calibration, battery cycling, suspend/cpufreq/cpuidle, shutdown/reboot changes,
audio playback and Linux hardware operations.

## Expected files/components

`docs/knowledge/power.md`, relevant cross-references in `audio-path.md` and
`open-unknowns.md`; evidence locators for vendor PMIC/PWRAP and matching upstream
MFD, regmap, regulator and power_supply components. Do not assert sibling-SoC
compatibility without register/transport evidence; no code edits.

## Acceptance criteria

Transport/driver identity and each scoped rail have CONFIRMED/INFERRED/UNKNOWN
status with locators. Battery duplicate fields are explained or explicitly
unresolved; no fabricated physical measurement. One future read-only probe has
specified expected result, register/metadata safety and required prerequisites,
or the document identifies a bounded blocker. Shared supply ownership is clear
enough that later research will not propose disabling another consumer's rail.

## Validation

Reconcile stock bindings with FM call sites and exact upstream v6.18 support;
verify units and distinguish framework output from hardware telemetry. Document
all denials. Documentation/evidence consistency checks only in this issue.

## Stop conditions

Stop at any need for live PMIC/debug register reads of unknown side effects,
rail/charge writes, uncertain units or ambiguous shared ownership. Preserve
inherited power state and record the missing fact.

## Unlocks next

reusable supply prerequisites for audio without starting audio bring-up.
